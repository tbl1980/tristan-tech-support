FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

No tech knowledge
required.

A few straightforward answers before we get started.

I don’t know much about technology. Can you still help?

Absolutely. You don’t need the right technical words. Explain what you’re trying to do and what happens instead. I’ll ask questions, explain the options clearly, and work at a pace that feels comfortable.

Who do you work with?

Individuals, families, home offices, and small businesses are welcome. Tell me what you need so I can confirm it fits my experience and availability. If another specialist is a better fit, I’ll be honest about that.

Where and when are you available?

I’m based in Edmond, Oklahoma, with appointments after 5 p.m. Central on weekdays and by arrangement on weekends. Edmond on-site travel is included in the listed visit rate. Other Oklahoma City metro locations are considered by arrangement, with any travel cost agreed beforehand. Suitable issues can also be handled remotely.

How much will it cost?

Remote support is $75 per hour with a 30-minute minimum. On-site support is $95 per hour with a one-hour minimum. Both are billed in 15-minute increments after the minimum. Some setups have package prices, and repairs or larger projects receive a quote. See Services & Pricing for the details.

Are evenings and weekends extra?

No. Evening and weekend appointments are my normal availability, so there is no extra surcharge during those hours. Appointments depend on my schedule; this is not a 24/7 emergency service.

Can you fix my Wi-Fi, printer, computer, or phone?

I can help with network setup and troubleshooting, computers, printers, and phone issues. What’s possible depends on the device and the cause. Share the make, model, and symptoms if you know them. Repairs and replacement parts are assessed before you approve the work.

Should I buy parts before contacting you?

It’s usually best to talk first. I can help check compatibility and whether a purchase is worthwhile. Parts and software are not included in labor rates; costs and purchasing arrangements are agreed before ordering.

What if my device can’t be repaired?

I’ll explain what I found and whether repair, replacement, or a specialist is the more sensible next step. Approved diagnostic work and hourly minimums still apply, even when repair isn’t possible. I won’t start additional paid work without discussing it with you.

Can you recover my files?

I can assess file-transfer and backup options, but recovery is not guaranteed. If a drive may have failed, describe the symptoms before continuing to use it. Cases that need specialist recovery should go to a dedicated recovery provider.

Do I need to share my password?

Don’t send passwords, payment details, or sensitive files through the contact form. If access is needed during an appointment, we’ll discuss it then; you can enter credentials yourself. Back up important files before repair work when possible.

How do I book?

Send a message through the Contact page or email me with the issue, your location, and a preferred evening or weekend time. I’ll reply to discuss the next step. Sending a request does not confirm an appointment.

Not sure where your question fits?

That’s okay. Start with what you know.
